Design a distinctive flat black logo symbol: an abstract angular MOTH, for a serious creative software company.
This is a name-independent symbol for a broad media-tools platform. No lettering or wordmark.
The design must combine the precise economy of a developer-tool icon with the memorable personality of a small creature. It is NOT a cartoon. No face, eyes, mouth, legs or antennae. The moth is recognizable entirely through its wing silhouette.
Design direction: broad, dense, chiseled upper wings with clipped blunt tips, much smaller lower wings tucked near the body, and an extremely reduced center. The whole mark has a compact, near-square footprint, slightly wider than tall. Use a confident near-symmetric silhouette with one subtle deliberate asymmetry or chamfer as a signature. ONE united filled silhouette, with two clean negative-space cuts defining the wing hinge. The inner cuts are wide enough to survive at 24px and stop before splitting the entire mark into scattered parts. No thin strokes. No spindly body. No long central projection. No elaborate veins. No repeated tiny details.
Treat this as an excellent modernist graphic emblem or a mature independent creative-tool brand, not a sports/esports insignia. It should be immediately recognizable and a little unusual, calm and assured rather than aggressive. Let the personality come from the wing proportions and silhouette. Carefully balance ink weight and empty space. The angled wings must not read as arrows, chevrons, a lightning bolt, a letter M, or a generic butterfly clipart icon. Avoid four equally sized diamond petals, stars, sparkles, circles, badges and enclosing boxes. Avoid toy-like, glossy, mascot or origami illustration treatments.
Color: solid near-black #202020 on pure white.
Presentation: one square 1024×1024 canvas, no text. Place ONE large resolved emblem in the center at approximately 330px wide, with extensive white space. Near the bottom center, repeat precisely the same emblem once at 32px wide as a small-size proof. Use exactly the same geometry in both sizes. Flat vector-like edges. No grids, mockups, textures, gradients, shadows, labels, or decoration.
Make the mark itself strong enough to stand alone. Reduce until there are no unnecessary corners.
