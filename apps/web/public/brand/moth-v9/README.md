# Angular moth, identity study

A name-independent creature silhouette for a broader media-tools platform. The mark has broad clipped upper wings, compact lower wings, two wing-hinge cuts, and a slightly different right wingtip. It has no face or lettering.

The original image-generation exploration was redrawn as an SVG, with a narrower footprint and larger lower wings. The vector is the reference artwork. The generated image is retained as exploration, not as the production master.

- `presentation.png` and `presentation.svg`: vector-based presentation.
- `symbol-black.svg`, `symbol-orange.svg`, `symbol-white.svg`: self-contained vector masters with transparent backgrounds.
- Matching PNG files: transparent 512-pixel exports.
- `symbol-16.png`, `symbol-24.png`, `symbol-32.png`: actual-size raster checks.
- `size-check.png`: nearest-neighbor enlargements of those three checks, left to right.
- `exploration.png`: initial built-in image-generation output.
- `design-prompt.txt`: complete generation prompt.

Prefer 24 pixels or larger when the wing cuts need to remain visible. At 16 pixels the silhouette remains, but the fine cuts become less distinct. Keep the mark upright and preserve the clipped right wingtip. Colors are charcoal `#202020`, orange `#FA4B00`, and white.

This is a static identity study. It has not replaced the website's current logo or been animated.
