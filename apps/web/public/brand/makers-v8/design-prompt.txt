Use case: logo-brand.
Design ONE original standalone emblem for a media-tools company whose name is not yet settled. The client says: "It's too generic and looks like an arrow. Keep it simple and sharp, but give it some personality."
The key is CHARACTER WITHOUT A FACE. This should feel closer to a distinctive modernist maker's hallmark or a small independent creative-tool brand than a stock SaaS logo. Be graphically bold and a little odd, but exceptionally reduced. No literal metaphor is required.
Invent a compact, dense, upright glyph from one confident black mass and one unusual internal notch or bite. It should have blunt, mostly right-angled terminals, a slightly offbeat asymmetry, strong visual rhythm and one memorable geometric surprise. Think of a custom printer's mark with attitude, not an alphabet letter, pictogram or corporate tech monogram. A chunky, precisely cut stamp that could be recognized from silhouette alone. ONE dominant contiguous form, not scattered pieces. Favor squat proportions and a grounded stance; it must not point or imply travel in any direction. At most one small controlled curve is allowed inside the otherwise angular geometry. The visual wit should come from a clever solid/empty relationship and proportions.
Avoid specifically: arrows, arrowheads, chevrons, shuttle shapes, speed lines, diagonal sports insignia, lightning bolts, slashes, tilted parallelograms, spinners, swooshes, rings, portals, sparkles, stars, cubes, frames, enclosing squares, circular badges, gears, play buttons, ordinary brackets, generic equalizer bars, mountains, flames, animals, eyes, mouths, cartoon faces, letters B/G/M, initials, recognizable existing brand symbols, decorative details. Do not make another arrow-like silhouette. No cut-paper illustration. No 3D.
The symbol should be sharp and simple but not symmetrical and sterile. It should look deliberately drawn by an excellent identity designer, not assembled from a common icon library. Do not explain it with text. The design must work on its own.
Presentation: a very sparse, plain #FAFAFA 3:2 landscape canvas. One charcoal #262626 emblem centered in the upper two-thirds, about 180 pixels high on a 1536-pixel-wide image, with generous empty space. A single very fine pale-gray divider below it. Below the divider, repeat EXACTLY THE SAME EMBLEM in orange #FA4B00, then much smaller in charcoal as a favicon-size proof, then white on a plain charcoal rectangular swatch. These are consistent applications of one design, never different concepts.
Absolutely no text, wordmark, labels, claims, slogans, construction grids, mockups, background patterns, gradients, shadows, texture, gloss or perspective. Flat vector-like solid artwork. Make the actual emblem more interesting than the presentation.
