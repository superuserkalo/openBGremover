Three directions for a platform that transforms images, audio and video. Each starts with something the tools actually work with.

01Splice

A precise cut through two pieces of media. The simplest direction, with a seam that could open and close in motion.

02Frames

A compact composition of frames. Connects to images, video and the way several media tools work together.

03Raster

Scan lines and sampled data. A more technical direction with a rhythm that could shift as media is processed.
