Use case: logo-brand.
Act as a skilled identity designer exploring highly reduced original logo symbols for openBGremover, a media API starting with background removal. The client likes the precise compactness of Cursor and the distinct personality of Firecrawl, but wants NO face, mascot, cute character, flame, eyes, or cartoon. They rejected generic spinning ribbons, random two-piece lightning-like polygons, and clunky wobbling animation. Do not reuse those ideas.
Create a beautifully composed comparison sheet with THREE genuinely DIFFERENT static logo concepts, A, B, C, all black on a completely plain white background. No animation, no movement lines, no 3D, no gradients, no shadows, no mockups. This is a mark-design study, not a brand presentation or website.
Each mark must be exceptionally simple and recognizable at 24 pixels, with a compact clear silhouette and a deliberate memorable quirk in the geometry. Personality comes from proportion and visual wit. Use confident filled forms, crisp cuts and intentional negative space. Each symbol must have a clear visual idea rather than being arbitrary shards.
Direction A: an original lowercase b / open-circle monogram, with negative space intelligently opening one counter. Avoid conventional font glyphs or a letter placed in a box. Quiet, dense, precise.
Direction B: an original foreground-cutout symbol. A single recognisable compact object-like abstract silhouette, with one decisive negative-space cut suggesting removing what surrounds the subject. No scissors, erasers, outlines, photo icon, rectangle frame, arrow, or geometric animal. Seek wit and economy.
Direction C: an original pixel-boundary symbol. A compact interplay of solid and empty stepped areas suggesting a foreground emerging from a transparent checkerboard, reduced to the fewest meaningful forms. Avoid a generic cluster of floating squares or checkerboard icon. Its outer silhouette should be singular and strong.
Composition: landscape 3:2 canvas, generous empty margins. Three equally sized columns, each containing ONE main black symbol in the upper-middle and exactly the same symbol repeated much smaller below it as a favicon-size proof. A small understated letter A, B or C below each column is the only text. All main symbols should have approximately equal optical size, about 140px tall on a 1536px-wide canvas, and all small repeats should be about 24px tall. Refined spacing and plenty of white space. Do not add paragraphs, captions, names or dividers. Do not make all three marks look similar.
Put the design effort into original, coherent logo geometry. Avoid gratuitous complexity. These should look like considered early logo studies from a good independent studio, not stock tech logos.
