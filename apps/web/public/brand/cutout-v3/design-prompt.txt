Use case: logo-brand.
Create a new professional logo concept for "openBGremover", a media-processing API for apps and AI agents whose first operation is background removal.
The user wants the COMBINATION of precise angular developer-tool geometry and the memorable playful personality of a mascot. Previous proposals of a square frame and a curled circular spinner were rejected. This must be a genuinely different identity.
Art direction: design a tiny "cutout sprite", an abstract origami-like character made from one strong asymmetric angular orange silhouette. Think a crisp folded piece of foreground paper that has come alive. The shape should have confidence and attitude, broad diagonal planes, 45-degree cuts, and only two tiny negative-space eyes as its character detail. Eyes are integrated into the geometry, slightly directional, not large cartoon eyes. Keep it charming, graphic and adult. Its silhouette should be distinctive before you notice the eyes. It should be visually dense and legible as a 24px mark. One or two filled shapes maximum, plus eyes. Use straight edges and a few optically softened joins, not a complex 3D illustration. An original faceted paper creature, not a literal animal, ghost, robot head, fire, flame, fox, cat, cube, standard mouse pointer, lightning bolt, star, aperture, ring, swoosh, or spinner. Do not copy any existing company's mark. Avoid the generic rounded square with two eyes. Do not add limbs, fingers, accessories, or outline strokes.
Motion potential is integral: the eyes can glance and blink; one small folded tip can flick; the whole symbol can lean and spring back. These gestures must preserve the core silhouette. Do not visually rotate it like a loading spinner.
Create ONE resolved logo identity on a plain #FAFAFA presentation sheet, landscape 3:2. Main upper composition: the original orange (#FA4B00) cutout-sprite symbol to the left of a charcoal (#262626) carefully kerned geometric sans wordmark reading exactly "openBGremover". Medium or semibold weight, understated. Large clean logo, abundant white space.
Lower quarter separated with one fine light-gray rule: three small instances of exactly the same icon geometry, showing neutral eyes, a subtle sideways glance, and a blink with a slight playful tilt. No arrows or motion graphics. One instance may be white on charcoal to show monochrome usage. Lower icons should visibly belong to exactly the same identity, not unrelated alternative designs.
Only text anywhere is the main wordmark "openBGremover". No headings, captions, slogans, fake UI, construction grids, mockups, gradients, shadows, textures, 3D rendering, glossy effects, or watermarks. Crisp flat vector-like edges. Prioritize the quality and originality of the actual logo over the presentation.
