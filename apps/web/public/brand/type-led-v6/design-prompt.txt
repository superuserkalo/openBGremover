Use case: logo-brand.
Create a genuinely new TYPE-LED brand identity concept for "openBGremover", an open-source media-processing API for apps and AI agents. The client is unhappy with previous geometric icons, star cutouts in squares, spinners, lightning-like shards, and mascots. This is a decisive pivot to typography.
Main idea: a custom-drawn wordmark is the identity. Write the exact full name "openBGremover" on one line. Give it recognizable, deliberate letter construction rather than selecting a generic ready-made font. A precise, contemporary humanist grotesk: sturdy strokes, clean terminals, open apertures, balanced proportions, restrained optical corrections, slightly unexpected but very readable. Combine technical sharpness with warmth through letter proportions. It should have the clarity of an excellent developer-tool brand and the character of a good independent type foundry. Neither sci-fi stencil nor cute rounded lettering.
The central capitals "BG" are the signature: invent a simple, elegant shared-stem or counter relationship between B and G, with one intentional opening suggesting a background being removed. Preserve unmistakable B and G letter recognition. Integrate this detail naturally into the full wordmark; do not put BG in a box, circle, badge, tile, or pill. Avoid gratuitous slices across the whole name. Do not add an icon to the left of the primary wordmark.
Develop a compact standalone BG ligature directly from those SAME two custom letters for favicon use. It must look clearly related to the actual lettering, not like an unrelated abstract tech symbol. The ligature must remain readable and structurally simple, with no more than two joined letter forms.
Composition: refined landscape 3:2 presentation on plain pale #FAFAFA. Main upper 60% shows the charcoal #262626 custom full wordmark, large but with generous empty margins. Lower portion separated by a single fine light-gray rule: on the left show the small BG ligature at about 100px high in charcoal; center show precisely the same ligature at a realistic small icon size; right show the full wordmark much smaller in white on a plain charcoal horizontal strip, as a restrained header-size proof. Orange #FA4B00 may appear only once on the larger BG ligature if it helps the identity. Every repeat must use identical lettering geometry.
The ONLY text in the image is "openBGremover" in the full-wordmark positions and the BG letter ligature. No labels, slogans, paragraphs, or mock UI controls.
Flat vector-like artwork, exceptionally clean edges, simple solid colors. No gradients, shadows, textures, 3D, animated poses, cartoon faces, stars, sparkles, frames, cubes, arrows, flames, aperture symbols, abstract ribbons, folded paper, or decoration. The entire design effort goes into the original custom typography, not a presentation template.
