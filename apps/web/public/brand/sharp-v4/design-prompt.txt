Use case: logo-brand.
Create a sharply reduced original logo for "openBGremover", an open-source media-processing API for apps and AI agents.
The user explicitly asks: "simpler and sharper, with personality but without the cartoon face."
Design a single compact abstract mark inspired by a crisp piece of foreground being cut free. Two angular interlocking planes maximum. A bold oblique silhouette with an unexpected clean negative-space incision, asymmetry and a sense of forward intention. Think a distinctive cut-paper glyph: a solid, confident form, not an illustration. The personality must come only from the proportions, angle and one off-center cut. Flat, geometric, clever, severely edited. Legible at 20 pixels. No enclosing square or circle. No frame. No star. No flame. No spinner or aperture. No folded-paper animal. Absolutely no eyes, face, mouth, character, mascot, ears, limbs, cartoon shapes, standard mouse pointer, generic lightning bolt, chevron pair or play button. Do not copy Cursor or Firecrawl. Precise diagonals, crisp corners, no outline strokes. Make the empty cut a meaningful part of the silhouette.
Use charcoal #262626 as the primary symbol color. Use orange #FA4B00 only for a smaller secondary application. The form must work in one ink.
Present ONE resolved identity on a pale #FAFAFA wide 3:2 sheet with abundant negative space. Main upper two thirds: moderately sized charcoal symbol left of the exact wordmark "openBGremover" in a carefully kerned restrained semibold geometric sans. The symbol should be about the height of the capital letters plus 30%, not a huge illustration overwhelming the name. Below one fine light-gray divider, show the identical standalone mark in charcoal, orange, and white on a small charcoal field. No alternate concepts, no text beyond the one exact main wordmark. No gradients, textures, shadows, dimensional shading, 3D, mockups, grids, captions or labels.
Choose a mark whose small component could shift 3 pixels or whose whole form could tilt 8 degrees on hover, while always preserving the identity. Do not draw movement lines. The still logo must carry the idea on its own.
